## Pilot Re-Analysis: Why the Internal Report Was Wrong

The internal pilot report headline said:

> *Served by the agent: 40.1% | All other users: 17.7% → 2.3× uplift*

This comparison is **biased (selection bias)**: the agent only served users who opened the app (already more active). Comparing them to everyone else — including users who never opened the app — inflates the apparent uplift.

### Correct ITT (all treatment vs all holdout)

| Group | N | Success Rate |
|-------|---|-------------|
| Treatment arm | 1912 | 18.2% |
| Holdout arm | 1979 | 19.6% |

**Correct uplift:** -1.3 pp (95% CI: [-3.8, 1.2])

### Biased Comparison (as in the internal report)

| Group | N | Success Rate |
|-------|---|-------------|
| Served users | 217 | 40.1% |
| All other users | 3674 | 17.7% |

**Biased uplift:** +22.4 pp

*Why biased:* The agent serves only users who triggered it by opening the app. These users are already more active and more likely to convert — so comparing them to everyone else (including inactive users who never opened the app) inflates the apparent uplift. This is selection bias, not a treatment effect.

## Limitations

- This replay has only ~2,769 triggers; small samples mean wide confidence intervals.
- Outcome attribution is limited to events generated by the simulator (14-day window).
- True ITT would require outcome data for ALL arm members, including those who never triggered.
- G11 (tv_activation) outcomes may be incomplete as tv_app_open is rarely generated.
